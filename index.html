Data Driven Smart Capital Allocation
AI that reads, compares and ranks investment memoranda so capital goes where it returns most.

PROBLEM

Investment memoranda above $10M reach HQ with 3,000+ data points each, all reviewed by hand. Review takes about 7 working days, projects are hard to compare, and ranking under constrained capital rests on uneven information.

SOLUTION

AI reads each investment memorandum and structures its data, surfacing key assumptions and sources. It matches values to Power BI production data, compares projects against historical analogues, then ranks them consistently against Group targets when capital is constrained. Reviewers focus on material risks and decisions, not data gathering.

EXPECTED IMPACT

  • •~1% uplift in Group portfolio return over five years
  • •5% cut in annual Canada CAPEX of $270M, about $13.5M a year (AED 50M)
  • •3,000+ data points per memorandum, structured and compared on one standard
